# Coverage pilot: inspection notes

The frozen pilot is complete: six selected questions × three modes × two repeats, with all 36 case-runs scored. These notes preserve observations made during execution and distinguish model quality, structural checks and evaluator failure.

**Decision: retain source support as the production default.** Off passed 7/12, support 10/12 and support+coverage 8/12. The paired outcomes favor support for this prototype; six selected tasks do not establish statistical superiority or general reliability. Coverage remains available as an experiment. Total returned-usage estimate: $9.6452267, including the judge. [Full comparison](summary.json) · [Independent final accounting](accounting-review-final.json)

Independent review of the first off arm is in [off-first-review.json](off-first-review.json). It checked actual answers/actions and all 14 read source passages across the two failures. For cap04, the missing project argument is a real setup omission; the mandatory weave.Evaluation API is too narrow because the question can permit documented EvaluationLogger. Accepting that alternative alone would not turn the case into a pass. The malformed `[S8-UI details…]` marker is also ignored by the strict citation-ID extractor; the cited S8 passage is an evaluation guide, while the UI claim belongs to an unread remote-scorer section. No score or case was changed mid-run.

Support cap06 required a repair: the initial draft attached a claim about top-level start_turn and grouping without an active conversation to a passage that did not establish it. The verifier requested removal or a narrower sourced formulation; the revised answer passed. That mechanism costs additional calls and does not by itself establish a higher case pass rate than the unchecked answer. Actual backend nesting and eval identity are verified in [agent-trace-readback-v2.json](../agent-trace-readback-v2.json).

The three first-repeat workflow failures are inspected together in [workflow-review.json](workflow-review.json). The tracing omission was already present in the initial drafts despite relevant text being available. No arm read the remote-scorer page's explicit asynchronous-scoring section. The source checker repaired provenance gaps, but support-only also softened the timing advice. The judge sometimes described stronger after-client-delivery ordering than the selected docs establish. Coverage's smaller page count is diagnostic and did not fail the case. These distinctions prevent blaming every omission on the final check.

An actual coverage repair is recorded in [coverage-repair-review.json](coverage-repair-review.json). In cap05's TypeScript follow-up, eight claims were supported but one material lifecycle obligation was uncertain, so the check rejected the draft. Further reads and a revised example followed; the final independent judge passed all four assertions. However, the timing passage was already retained from the preceding turn, and both the draft and checker incorrectly called it unread. Availability of conversation evidence did not ensure correct use. Feedback also mixed coverage with provenance demands; the first draft was not independently judged. This establishes a repair mechanism, not a pure causal quality gain from coverage.

[accounting-review-t1.json](accounting-review-t1.json) independently recomputes first-repeat costs from 162 unique provider response IDs and checks all 21 saved agent turns against their case records. The total is $4.7891449 with no missing or unpriced returned usage. Assertion totals are micro-averages; Weave's default scalar summary averages per-case fractions. Agent latency sums all turns in a case and excludes judging/harness time. Public Compare opens, but support/coverage summary columns initially show N/A; this audit does not claim fully rendered public summaries.

## False refusals are failures too

The second coverage repeat declined two answerable questions. [coverage-abstention-review.json](coverage-abstention-review.json) shows a TypeScript follow-up exhausting its repair allowance after a claim was labeled partial despite relevant inherited source text. [preset-abstention-review.json](preset-abstention-review.json) shows the review-queue answer reaching a final audit with all ten listed claims supported and all four obligations covered, yet still receiving a failing verdict. A source-less statement about what the docs did not say also violated the checker schema. Neither failure proves the coverage checklist alone caused it: support judgments, evidence requirements and the hard repair limit interact.

The active online Signal tagged the TypeScript failure `needs-review` approximately 5.2 seconds after its turn ended. [Live Signal receipt](live-signal-review.json) links the actual turn, evaluation and configured monitor. That is one observed monitoring event, not a latency promise or proof that every failure is detected. Signal cost is outside the Anthropic estimate; the underlying scoring request was not available for usage verification.

[off-second-review.json](off-second-review.json) completes inspection of all 11 recorded failed case-runs. Two further unchecked answers cited source IDs never assigned by a read, even though optional facts appeared in search snippets. The workflow answers again omitted retrieved initialization and missed the explicit asynchronous section. All 31 passages checked in that review match the locked corpus. Evaluator API-choice and exact client-delivery-order limitations remain disclosed; no primary verdict was rewritten.

## Corrections after the frozen experiment

Code `179a052` adds a bounded Markdown-aware citation-format guard and changes rubric identity to `2.1-assertions`; the assertion judge is unchanged. Checked modes request repair for malformed source-like prose brackets. Ordinary links and code are excluded. Experimental off remains unchecked. A [deterministic replay](citation-replay-v2.1.json) found malformed citations in two of the 36 stored answers; it preserves the original answers and scores and makes no claim about counterfactual regenerated responses.

The exhausted-limit message now explains that incomplete research or an overstrict verifier may be responsible. It no longer asks the user to narrow an already clear question. The app also shows the original code, prompt, settings and documentation identities; early records remain explicitly unknown where those fields were never captured.

The [final accounting audit](accounting-review-final.json) reconciles 330 unique provider requests across 42 agent turns and 36 case-runs, with no missing or unpriced returned usage. Support and off both passed 39/44 assertions; support's better case total also reflects citation integrity. Coverage passed 32/44. Repeated assertions are correlated, not independent extra trials.

The [anonymous browser review](compare-ui-review.json) now shows all six case rows for both repeats. Some summary columns still report missing information. Use the recorded comparison links and per-case JSON alongside that limitation; no paid answer was repeated to improve the dashboard.
